Logo

A small landscape in a circle: rolling hills under a warm sun, a garden path, and a fern frond in the foreground. It says what the business does, turning a plot of land into a place to enjoy. Use the mark alone for favicons and social avatars. Keep clear space of half the mark's width around it. The wordmark text in the SVG files uses Fraunces and Karla; outline the text in a vector editor before sending to a printer.
